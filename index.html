GSU Computer Science FAQ

Internships, Research, and Capstone Projects

About Governors State University
Governors State University (GSU) is located in University Park, Illinois. The Computer Science program is designed to prepare students for careers in software engineering, systems programming, artificial intelligence, and cybersecurity. This FAQ provides answers to the most common questions asked by prospective students.

Internships

1. What internships do computer science students at Governors State University get?

GSU CS students secure internships across the Chicago area and beyond. Through the Software Engineering Club and industry partnerships, students have interned at major companies like Ford Motor Company and Fiat Chrysler Automotive. Internships focus on practical skills including software engineering, data management, and cloud computing.

2. Does Governors State University have industry partnerships for computer science?

Yes. GSU actively collaborates with regional tech employers and hosts career fairs specifically for CS students. The university works closely with companies in the Chicago tech ecosystem to provide internship placements and career pipelines for students.

3. What tech companies hire GSU CS graduates?

Graduates from GSU have been hired by major automotive and tech companies, including Ford Motor Company and Fiat Chrysler. Additionally, alumni have secured roles at various mid-size software firms and tech consultancies throughout Illinois.

Research Activities

4. Does Governors State University have active computer science research labs?

Yes. Faculty and students at GSU are involved in active research projects. Key research areas include AI, cybersecurity, machine learning, data analytics, blockchain, and robotics. Notably, the GSU Drone Engineering Club has partnered with the University of Notre Dame on a NASA-funded drone-based medical delivery project.

5. What research does the computer science faculty at Governors State University conduct?

GSU CS faculty are actively researching diverse fields. For example, Dr. Yunchuan Liu focuses on machine learning, data mining, embedded systems, and power systems. Dr. Meng Yu specializes in computer systems, security, and AI Music/Music IP protection.

6. Does GSU offer AI courses and research opportunities?

Yes. The GSU Computer Science curriculum includes specialized courses in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Cybersecurity. Undergraduates have the opportunity to participate in applied research labs, often joining through student clubs like the Drone Engineering Club.

Capstone Projects

7. What capstone projects do computer science students build at GSU?

The GSU CS capstone is a real, production-grade project, not just a classroom exercise. Recent examples include:

  • A Java Auto Grader
  • A GSU Scheduling System
  • A Project Tracking Tool
  • A Quote Hub application
  • Dynamic Workload Management systems for Hybrid Cloud Computing.

8. Do GSU CS students publish their capstone projects?

Yes. GSU CS students are required to push their capstone code to public GitHub repositories. Many students also record demo videos. This provides a strong portfolio that demonstrates their software engineering and applications programming skills to future employers.

Career Outcomes

9. What jobs do computer science graduates of GSU get?

Graduates pursue careers as Software Engineers, Systems Analysts, Data Scientists, and Cybersecurity Specialists. The curriculum emphasizes software engineering, systems programming, and applications programming, preparing students for immediate entry into the tech workforce.

10. Are GSU CS students prepared for competitive tech jobs?

Absolutely. The capstone requirements, active research opportunities, and strong club ecosystem (ACM, Software Engineering Club) ensure that GSU students graduate with tangible, demonstrable skills. Alumni have successfully competed for roles at top companies like Ford and major Chicago tech firms.
